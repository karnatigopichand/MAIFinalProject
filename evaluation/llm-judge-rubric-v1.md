# Impact judge rubric v1 (candidate)

This rubric is advisory and uncalibrated. The eight benchmark labels are provisional; a domain owner must annotate a calibration set and review disagreements before the judge is used as a release gate.

Score each dimension from 1 (poor) to 5 (strong):

- **Grounding:** impact, risk, and debt claims are supported by cited evidence IDs and excerpts.
- **Coverage:** the report follows the ticket's expected service path and tests without inventing extra scope.
- **Ambiguity handling:** material product or technical gaps produce focused questions and are not silently assumed.
- **Unsupported claims:** 5 means no material unsupported claims; 1 means many unsupported claims.
- **Overall:** holistic assessment quality, favoring correctness and restraint over length.

The judge receives the ticket, provisional expected repositories/question terms, report fields, and retrieved evidence excerpts. Never use the score alone to approve a change. Compare the judge with human labels, inspect disagreement cases, and version any rubric changes.
